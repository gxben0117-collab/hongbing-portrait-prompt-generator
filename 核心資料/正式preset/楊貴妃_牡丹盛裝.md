# 楊貴妃 · 牡丹盛裝

## 定位

- 類型：朝代宮服 / 唐朝
- 角色：楊貴妃風格
- 場景：牡丹盛裝 / 宮廷花苑

## 測試用途

此 preset 用於驗證：

- 頭飾與髮量增加時是否仍維持合理頭身比例
- 盛妝狀態下是否仍保留真人臉部結構
- 華麗宮廷服裝是否有足夠肩頸支撐，不出現頭過大感

## 範例方向

- scene：Tang dynasty imperial peony garden, marble balustrades, warm imperial afternoon, ceremonial floral richness
- costume：rose-gold Tang court robe, wide sleeves, layered imperial silhouette, peony hair ornaments under controlled volume
- action：stable three-quarter classical pose guided by architecture, hands low, face fully visible
- camera：vertical three-quarter hanfu portrait, face clear, costume silhouette legible, shoulder support readable beneath the styling
